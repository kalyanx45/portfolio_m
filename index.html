About

Cuts that keep them watching.

From concept to final export, I handle the full post-production pipeline: rough cuts, color grading, motion graphics, sound design, and delivery across every platform. Whether it's a 15-second TikTok or a 20-minute brand documentary, I bring the same obsessive attention to pacing, rhythm, and story.
